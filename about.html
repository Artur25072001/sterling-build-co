About Us

The Story Behind Sterling Build Co.

12 years. Hundreds of projects. One simple commitment: do the job right, every time.

Built on Old-School Values

Sterling Build Co. started in 2013 with a pickup truck, a tool belt, and a belief that the construction industry could do better. Too many homeowners were dealing with missed deadlines, surprise invoices, and contractors who stopped answering the phone once the deposit cleared. We set out to change that.

Our founder spent over a decade on the tools before launching Sterling — working on everything from high-end custom homes to multi-unit residential projects. That hands-on experience shaped how we operate: every estimate is detailed line-by-line, every timeline is realistic, and every crew member shares the same commitment to quality.

Today, Sterling Build Co. is a tight-knit team of licensed carpenters, project managers, and trusted subcontractors — many of whom have been with us for years. We take on a select number of projects at a time so every client gets the attention they deserve.

We're not the biggest contractor in the GTA, and we don't want to be. We want to be the one people trust enough to recommend to their parents, their neighbours, and their best friends.
