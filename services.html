What We Do

Full-Service Construction & Renovation Across the GTA

From concept to completion, we handle every phase of your project. No subcontractor roulette. No finger-pointing. Just one team, one point of contact, one result you'll be proud of.

Our Specialty

Kitchen & Bathroom Renovations

Kitchens and bathrooms aren't just the most-used rooms in your home — they're also the most complex to renovate. Plumbing, electrical, ventilation, waterproofing, cabinetry, and tile all need to work together seamlessly. We've done hundreds of them, and we know every detail that can make or break a project.

Our process starts with understanding how you actually use your space. Then we design a layout that works for your daily routine — not just for a magazine spread. Custom cabinetry, quartz or granite countertops, tile selection, fixture placement, lighting design — we guide you through every decision.

Typical timeline: 4–8 weeks for a full kitchen, 2–4 weeks for a bathroom. We keep the site clean, communicate daily, and never leave you without a functioning sink longer than necessary.

  • Full gut renovations and cosmetic updates
  • Custom cabinetry, countertops, and backsplashes
  • Floor plan reconfiguration and open-concept layouts
  • Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC rough-in

Expand Your Space

Home Additions

Outgrowing your home doesn't always mean moving. A well-designed addition gives you the space you need without the stress, cost, and disruption of selling and buying. We specialize in additions that look like they were always part of the original structure — not tacked on as an afterthought.

Second-storey additions, main-floor bump-outs, sunrooms, and in-law suites — we've done them all. Our team handles everything from architectural drawings and permits to foundation work, framing, roofing, and interior finishing.

We know that living through a major addition is challenging. We'll work with you to phase the construction so you can stay in your home as much as possible, and we'll give you a realistic timeline upfront — no sugar-coating.

  • Second-storey additions and bungalow expansions
  • Main-floor bump-outs for kitchens and living areas
  • In-law suites and basement apartment conversions
  • Full permit and architectural drawing management

Outdoor Living

Custom Decks & Outdoor Living

A great deck isn't just a platform with some railings — it's an extension of your home. We design and build outdoor spaces that handle Canadian winters and make the most of our all-too-short summers. From pressure-treated pine to premium cedar and composite, we'll help you choose materials that fit your budget and maintenance preferences.

Beyond the deck itself, we can integrate pergolas, privacy screens, built-in seating, planter boxes, outdoor kitchens, and low-voltage lighting. Every project is designed to meet Ontario Building Code requirements for footings, railings, and structural loads.

We handle the permit process from start to finish — including any variances that may be required for your property. You focus on planning the first barbecue; we'll handle the rest.

  • Multi-level decks, ground-level decks, and rooftop terraces
  • Pergolas, gazebos, and covered outdoor structures
  • Built-in seating, planters, and privacy screens
  • Outdoor kitchens and low-voltage landscape lighting

Your Project, Managed

General Contracting

Some projects don't fit neatly into a single category. Maybe you're finishing a basement, opening up a load-bearing wall, or doing a whole-home renovation in phases. That's where our general contracting service comes in.

As your general contractor, we take full responsibility for the project from day one. We coordinate every trade — electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, drywallers, painters, tilers — and make sure the schedule runs smoothly. We handle permits, inspections, and all the paperwork that comes with a major renovation.

You get one point of contact, one detailed invoice, and one team that's accountable for the entire outcome. No passing the buck, no "that's the other guy's problem."

  • Basement finishing and apartment conversions
  • Whole-home renovations and structural modifications
  • Interior demolition and load-bearing wall removal
  • Permit management, inspections, and trade coordination
