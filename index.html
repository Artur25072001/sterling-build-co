Who We Are

A Contractor You Can Trust — No Surprises, Just Solid Work

Sterling Build Co. was founded on a simple belief: every homeowner deserves a renovation experience that's as stress-free as the finished product is beautiful. We're not here to cut corners or push unnecessary upgrades. We're here to listen, plan carefully, and execute with precision.

With 12 years of hands-on experience across the Greater Toronto Area, we've built a reputation for showing up on time, staying on budget, and delivering work that lasts. Fully licensed and insured, we treat every project — big or small — like it's our own home.
